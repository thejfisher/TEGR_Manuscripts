## Overview

This repository serves as the canonical open-access archive and chronological research notebook for the **Teleparallel Equivalent of General Relativity (TEGR) Resonant Defect Framework**. 

Rather than treating spacetime curvature ($R$) as an axiomatic starting point or postulating wave-particle duality as an irreducible mystery of quantum mechanics, this work explores gravity, electromagnetism, and quantum kinematics bottom-up from the torsional geometry of a flat Weitzenböck spacetime ($T$). Fundamental particles are modeled as localized **resonant topological wave defects** propagating through a continuous kinematic vacuum.

### Research Philosophy: The Living Scientific Trail
This repository is published not as a single retrospective textbook, but as an honest, chronological research trail. It documents every stage of scientific discovery:
1. **The Pure Spreadsheet Baseline:** The earliest proof that 10-dimensional teleparallel degrees of freedom can be calculated deterministically without complex Hilbert spaces is preserved in [`TEGR_10x10_Matrix_WORKING (1).xlsx`](TEGR_10x10_Matrix_WORKING%20(1).xlsx).
2. **Methodological Audits & Transparent Revisions:** Crucial milestones where early hypotheses were falsified or refined are recorded transparently—including the discovery in **MS06** that pure geometry acts only as a phase router and requires an active wave engine, the discovery in **MS09** that the vacuum is natively massless ($1/r^2$) without numerical damping, and the formal retraction in **MS12** of artificial annealing caused by legacy engine boundary bugs.
3. **The Emergent Synthesis:** Demonstrating how deterministic, local kinematic strain smoothly yields macroscopic quantum interference (**MS08**), Kaluza-Klein $1/r^3$ scaling (**MS11**), and nested cosmological horizons (**MS14**, **MS15**).
